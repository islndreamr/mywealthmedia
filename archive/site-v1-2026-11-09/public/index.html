About

My Wealth Media is a business operations and scaling consultancy based in San Diego, run by me, Megan Lemay. I spent eight years behind a chair as a master stylist before I ever looked at an org chart. I built a book from zero, reached six figures in my second year, and trained other stylists to build theirs. That work taught me how people actually buy, how they tell you what they need without saying it, and what it costs to be the entire product.

Then I moved into online business and found companies making real money with almost nothing holding them up. The CRM said the leads were being worked. The processor said the revenue was collected. The team said the task was done. The client said nobody called. The bank balance said something else entirely.

I became the person who walked in and made it work. Operations, sales infrastructure, client delivery, payroll, disputes, recruiting, training, CRM migrations, websites, AI implementation. I have led a division responsible for roughly a thousand clients. I have supported remote teams of about seventy across setting, closing, and fulfillment. I have rebuilt enrollment infrastructure at three in the morning because the alternative was a company failing quietly.

What I Do

01

The Diagnostic

A paid, fixed scope look at where the business is leaking. I work through collected revenue against actual deposits, refunds and disputes, processing and financing costs, commission timing, software spend, and who actually owns what. You get a written finding ranked by what each item is costing you, with the fix next to it.

02

Operations

After you know what is broken, something has to hold. SOPs people follow without being chased. A CRM that reflects real activity. Onboarding that does not live in one person's memory. Accountability that applies to leadership too.

03

Sales and Delivery

High ticket infrastructure from first touch through fulfillment. Setter and closer training, call review and scoring, pipeline hygiene, commission structure, and the handoff between the person who sold it and the person who has to deliver it.
