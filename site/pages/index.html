I learned business from the inside out.

Before My Wealth Media, I spent eight years building a six-figure career in the beauty industry. I managed my own clientele, built relationships that lasted years, mentored new professionals, and learned firsthand how much discipline it takes to run a profitable business.

Eventually, I became more interested in what made businesses work than in the work I was doing inside them. That curiosity took me into sales leadership, business operations and executive roles, where I managed everything from team onboarding and revenue reporting to financial controls, CRM infrastructure and client fulfillment.

I learned how to find the problems buried inside a growing company. The bottlenecks nobody notices until something breaks. The information living in someone's head. The processes everyone thinks are working because they've always been done that way.

Then AI changed what was possible. I started building the systems I used to have to coordinate across entire teams. Custom software, client portals, automated workflows, internal AI agents, and operating tools designed around how businesses actually function.

I love the messy middle of business. The part where you have a great offer, real customers and a growing team, but you know there's a better way to make everything work together. That's where I do my best work.
