Privacy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who this covers

This site is run by My Wealth Media LLC, based in San Diego, California.

What we collect

When you ask for a guide, take the quiz or book a call, we collect what you type into the form: your first name, your email address and, for the quiz, your score.

How we use it

To send you what you asked for, to follow up about working together, and to send occasional emails about free resources. You can unsubscribe from any email we send, or email megan@mywealthmedia.com and we will remove you.

Who we share it with

Leads and emails are handled in GoHighLevel. The site is hosted on Netlify. If we add a booking tool, it will be listed here.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set tracking cookies today. We may add Google Analytics later, and if we do, this page will say so.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing megan@mywealthmedia.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will change with it.
